The Journey

1. Collection

Discarded flowers collected from temples. Sorted to remove plastics.

2. Cleaning

Petals are cleaned and sun-dried naturally to preserve oils.

3. Pulverization

Dried petals are ground into a fine, aromatic uniform powder.

4. Creation

Hand-rolled by artisans into incense or processed into leather.

5. Final Product

Cured, packaged, and ready to return fragrance to your home.

Circular Innovations

Flagship

Fleather

Fungal strains transform flower fibers into a durable, animal-free leather alternative.

Zero Waste

Seed Paper

Stationery that grows into a plant when discarded. Embedded with Tulsi seeds.

Fashion

Natural Dyes

Non-toxic pigments extracted from Hibiscus and Marigold for the fashion industry.

Energy

Bio-Char

Woody stems carbonized into high-efficiency, low-smoke fuel for local vendors.
